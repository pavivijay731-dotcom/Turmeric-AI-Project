Turmeric AI
Project Overview
Turmeric AI is a web-based AI-assisted crop monitoring project focused on helping users understand common problems affecting turmeric crops through a simple and accessible digital platform.
The project is being developed as part of an AI Immersion Project, where the objective is to identify a genuine real-world problem through field observation and explore an AI-powered solution.

Problem / Real-World Observation

During a real turmeric field visit, problems affecting the crop were observed and discussed.

The major problems identified include:

- Unhealthy and drying turmeric leaves.
- Possible disease or pest-related damage.
- Water-management difficulties.
- Risk of affected plants and crop loss.

The project explores how an AI-assisted web application can help monitor turmeric crop health and provide useful information and recommendations.

Project Approach

The project follows a real-world problem-solving approach:

1. Visit the real location.
2. Observe and understand the agricultural problem.
3. Identify the major causes and challenges.
4. Design an AI-assisted solution.
5. Develop a simple web-based prototype.
6. Test and improve the solution based on further development and validation.
